# Puerto Rico Medicaid (Plan Vital / ASES) vs US Medicaid — cited comparison (DRAFT)

**Summary.** Puerto Rico's Medicaid is a capped block grant with a statutory 55% federal match. States get an open-ended match of 50%–83%. Congress has raised PR to 76% and set annual caps through FY2027 (Sept 30, 2027). As of Oct 2026 no extension has been enacted, so the "Medicaid cliff" lands Oct 1, 2027. PR spends **$3,293 per enrollee (FY2024)**: about one-third of the state average ($10,426) and about half of the lowest-spending state (Hawaii, $6,606). PR enrolls a far larger share of its population (about 40%, versus about 21% nationally), its income eligibility line is lower, and its primary-care shortage areas have a smaller share of their need met.

**Sources:** MACPAC MACStats (Feb 2026), KFF, CRS (IF11012, R47821), Medicaid.gov (CMS enrollment and PR SPA 23-0001), Census / PR Institute of Statistics, AAMC 2025, HRSA via KFF, El Nuevo Día / Metro PR (2026 status only).

## Caveats

- **Cliff dollar figure disagrees.** ENDI says about $500M under the permanent statute. Metro PR says federal funds fall from $4.7B to $1.7B. Neither is an official CMS or CBO number, and KFF/CRS give the method but no dollar figure. Use "a cut of billions per year" or quote one outlet by name.
- **Enrollment counts disagree.** ASES/officials report ~1.2M–1.36M (point-in-time). MACPAC reports 1.43M FYE (CMS-64, FY2024). The US count excludes territories and is for June 2026.
- **The per-enrollee gap is partly structural.** PR's program does not cover long-term services and supports (MACPAC 2019), and the MACStats Exh. 23 totals include DSH and waiver pool payments. MACPAC's own adjusted comparisons control for enrollment mix. Do not present this as a pure price gap.
- **HPSA population share cuts the other way.** About 18.0% of PR residents live in a designated primary-care HPSA, versus about 27.0% in the US (derived: 574,059 / 3,184,835 and 92.3M / 341.8M). The pitch-safe metric is **% of need met**, not % of population.
- **Mixed years.** FY2024 spending, 2025 population, June 2026 US enrollment, Dec 2025 HPSAs. Each row is labeled with its year.
- PR provider-payment figures come from statute (conditional) or 2020 press claims. They are not comparable to the KFF/Urban index.

## How to use in the pitch

1. "Puerto Rico Medicaid spends about $3,300 per enrollee, roughly one-third of the US state average and half of the lowest-spending state." (Section 2, MACPAC FY2024)
2. "About 4 in 10 Puerto Ricans rely on Plan Vital, versus about 1 in 5 Americans on Medicaid nationally." (Section 3, ENDI / CMS + Census, derived)
3. "Federal Medicaid funding for Puerto Rico is capped and faces a cliff on Oct 1, 2027: the match falls from 76% to 55% unless Congress acts." (Section 1, KFF / CRS)
4. "Puerto Rico's primary-care shortage areas have only 29% of their need met, against 48% nationally, and 41% of its physicians are 65 or older." (Section 5, HRSA via KFF; AAMC)
