
# Smart Queue Competitors

**Summary**: Waitlist backfill is a mature, crowded US product category, so Idea 3 cannot claim "backfill" as new. Nearly every tool sits on one practice's EHR or practice-management system, sells in English to US practices, and fills slots only inside one organization. No product was found that routes patients across a health plan's whole network of small independent PR offices, Spanish-first, with an audit log. That is the white space. **Update (2026-10-08):** two funded Puerto Rico companies, BrainHi and Citamed, already hold the schedule connection and the patient messaging in Spanish by voice and SMS; neither advertises refilling a cancelled slot, and either could add it. The standalone backfill companies elsewhere were absorbed into larger suites. The claim to keep is "network-wide, with a record of every move", not "Spanish by phone".

## Puerto Rico and Latin America

- **Doctoralia / Docplanner** operates in 13 countries (Poland, Brazil, Mexico, Spain, Italy, Germany, Turkey, Colombia, Czech Republic, Portugal, Argentina, Peru, Chile), **not Puerto Rico**. It is a marketplace with booking and reminders, not plan-wide backfill (source: idea-3-gap-research-urls-2026-10-07.txt → Docplanner) (vendor).
- **Docpoint**: a PR booking app where doctors posted offers on slow days (source: idea-3-gap-research-urls-2026-10-07.txt → Docpoint Metro PR 2015) (news, 2015). Current status unknown.
- **Triple-S**: the Mi Triple-S app (plan card, virtual visits, medicine delivery) plus MiConsulta MD. No in-network appointment booking was found (source: idea-3-gap-research-urls-2026-10-07.txt → Mi Triple-S app) (company app; "couldn't confirm" is not proof none exists).
- **MCS Classicare and MMM**: only provider directories and call centers were found (source: idea-3-gap-research-urls-2026-10-07.txt → search summary) (couldn't confirm; not proof none exists).
- **PR digital-health players found are mostly telemedicine** (Tu Salud Virtual, Smart Doctors), not scheduling (source: idea-3-gap-research-urls-2026-10-07.txt → search summary).

### Where the schedules live in Puerto Rico (added 2026-10-08)

- The government's health-information-exchange RFP (January 2024) names **NeoMed** ("significant market coverage"), **EHRez**, **Infomedika** and **Sabiamed** as the most used record systems; federally funded health centers use Greenway or eClinicalWorks (source: salud.pr.gov/CMS/DOWNLOAD/8442) (government document, read in full).
- **E Health Partners** (EHRez, 3,000 providers) acquired **Assertus Holdings** (ProClaim billing, 4,500 providers; Medicus, 1,000) in July 2025, so one group now reaches most independent offices (source: Health Connection PR, 2025-07-19) (news; counts are vendor-stated).
- None of the local systems publishes an integration programme, and none advertises a waitlist (negative finding, 2026-10-06; not proof of absence).
- Consequence for the design: no tool anywhere detects a freed slot without being the office's scheduling system or being connected to it. A production version depends on each vendor's programme, office by office. That is why the MVP takes the cancellation from staff and why "works without an EHR" is a real differentiator for small offices (derived).

### Spanish-language and standalone reference points (added 2026-10-08)

- **Doctoralia's offer mechanics**, useful as a design reference: on a cancellation, the slot is offered to 10 patients with visits in the next 48 hours, 10 minutes each, by SMS or WhatsApp, inside its own agenda (source: pro.doctoralia.es/productos/funcionalidades/lista-de-espera) (vendor, read 2026-10-06). Not in Puerto Rico.
- **WhatsApp receptionists in Spanish** exist in single countries: Serenna (Spain), Carecode (Brazil), Kosmo (Mexico), one patient at a time, "reply SÍ", inside a broader WhatsApp receptionist (vendor pages found in the 2026-10-06 pass; URLs not recorded, needs verification). None is in Puerto Rico, and WhatsApp cannot carry appointment details under the health privacy law (see the channel boundary in `../TECHNICAL_PROPOSAL.md`, §6).
- **The companies that sold backfill alone were absorbed.** QueueDr ran eight years on about $1M raised and sold to Phreesia in March 2021 for $10.1M; it survives as the Appointment Accelerator module (source: Phreesia's filing via MarketScreener) (measured on the price; "a feature, not a product" is a reading). Everseat was bought by Relatient in 2019 (press; needs verification). Waitly AI still sells a ranked waitlist alone to US specialty practices, by SMS and voice AI (vendor; needs verification).
- **Where the money is going:** AI front-desk agents that take the whole phone line (Assort Health above $100M raised, Hyro $95M, Hello Patient $22.5M); refill is one workflow inside them (trade press; unverified).
- **Patents (for the originality question):** US 11,416,828 (Publius Inc, active to 2039) covers ranking patients with later slots and swapping the first to accept, but its independent claim also requires offering the cancelling patient a telehealth visit with a video link, so a refill-only system does not match that text as written; a continuation issued in October 2025 was not read. US 11,640,585 covers a ranked waitlist queue. Prior art predates both (QueueDr dates from 2013) (source: Google Patents US11416828B2, US11640585B2) (claim text read 2026-10-06; this is a reading, not a legal opinion).

## White space (what Idea 3 can claim)

1. **One queue across a plan's whole network.** Kyruus/HealthSparq is directory plus booking. The NHS e-Referral Service (e-RS) lets patients pick the shortest wait (source: idea-3-gap-research-urls-2026-10-07.txt → NHS e-RS). Every US backfill tool works inside one organization. Routing new plan members to offices with open slots, while established patients stay with their own doctor, is not sold.
2. **No EHR required; works with phone bookings.** Small PR offices that book by phone or paper are not served. Zocdoc only adds new patients.
3. **Spanish-first, with a voice channel.** No vendor PR presence was found. PS 1263 named the elderly and chronically ill as most hurt by waits; the bill was recommended against and is not law (see [[ps-1263-pr-wait-times-bill]]), and older PR adults are less connected (see [[no-show-control-risks]]).
4. **An evidence / audit log of every move** (Julio's "evidencia"). No vendor sells a PR compliance or audit trail.
5. **Plan-visible access and capacity statistics across the network.** Incumbents report fill rates to the practice only.

(source: idea-3-gap-research-urls-2026-10-07.txt → search summary) (derived from the table above)

- **Pitch line**: "Backfill exists for big US health systems on Epic. Nobody does it for a health plan's network of small Puerto Rico offices, in Spanish, by phone, with a record of every move." (derived)
- Do not say "nobody does waitlist backfill". That is false.
- **Update (2026-10-08):** the load-bearing words in that line are "network" and "record of every move". "In Spanish, by phone" on its own is BrainHi's and Citamed's territory (table above); point 3 survives only as "Spanish-first **backfill**", and point 2 survives because the local record systems expose no integration programme (schedule section above).

## Caveats

- Every vendor performance number (Epic's 30 days, Phreesia's 40%, NexHealth's 20 minutes, Luma's 48% in [[waitlist-backfill]]) is unverified marketing.
- The PR search was not exhaustive. A plan-internal tool could exist without public coverage.
