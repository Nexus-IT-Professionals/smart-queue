## Puerto Rico landscape (added 2026-10-09)
- [[pr-front-desk-today]]: how Puerto Rico offices handle cancellations, no-shows and reschedules today: job postings, published policies, channels (phone and SMS, no WhatsApp), press and institutional record, dental offices, what the team heard from three offices.
- [[pr-office-systems]]: what the record and scheduling systems used in Puerto Rico do about appointments (NeoMed, EHRez, Infomedika, Sabiamed, Medicus and ProClaim, SecureEMR, Practice Fusion, BrainHi, Citamed); no local refill feature; no public API with appointments; evidence of which systems offices use.
- [[us-vendors-and-pr-barriers]]: the seven US waitlist vendors (integrations, Puerto Rico presence, price, Spanish) and seven barrier hypotheses with verdicts: integration dependency, reimbursement, SMS delivery, office size, decision-makers, order-of-arrival scheduling, referrals.
- [[pr-plans-and-access-standards]]: what the plans, ASES, Medicare Advantage Star Ratings and the Procurador del Paciente do about appointment access; the buyer for a network-wide queue and its regulatory clock.
